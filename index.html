The original explanation.

This presentation is an independent, paraphrased visual summary of Charles Lamanna's public post and 5:49 whiteboarding video about Work IQ and Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Watch Charles's video and read his post on LinkedIn ↗

The pricing proposal is his illustrative scenario. Diagram geometry and click groupings are adapted for this presentation; they are not a literal reproduction of his board or a product architecture specification.

About the numbers: in the video, Charles reports 80% fewer tokens used to generate a response and twice the response-generation speed with Work IQ APIs versus traditional APIs, across 20+ evaluations representing common scenarios.

These are source-reported findings, not independently reproduced measurements or promises for every workload. This page contains no customer data, embedded video, copied transcript, analytics, or external assets. It is not an official Microsoft product document.
